Culture Day Celebration
Organized by: Max Green Skill Development Organization under supervision of Directorate of Literacy and Non-Formal Education, Government of Sindh
Venue: Umerkot City
Max Green Skill Development Organization proudly organized a vibrant and memorable Culture Day Celebration in Umerkot City, marked by great enthusiasm, unity, and active participation. The event was a beautiful reflection of our rich cultural heritage and served as a meaningful platform to promote cultural awareness, preserve traditional values, and nurture the hidden talents of students.
The celebration witnessed enthusiastic participation from teachers, students, and staff members from all Max Green centers, making the event well-coordinated and truly impactful. The program commenced with an inspiring introductory session that emphasized the importance of culture, harmony, and unity in society. This thoughtful beginning set a positive and energetic tone for the entire event.
Students captivated the audience with a variety of colorful cultural performances, including melodious singing, traditional dances, and creative tableaus. Each performance beautifully portrayed the richness, diversity, and timeless traditions of our culture. The confidence, creativity, and dedication displayed by the students were highly appreciated by the audience and reflected their hard work and passion.
The role of teachers was commendable, as they provided continuous guidance, encouragement, and support to students during the preparation process. Their dedication played a vital role in shaping the performances and ensuring the overall success of the event.
Beyond entertainment, the Culture Day celebration served as a powerful learning experience. It helped students build self-confidence, enhance communication skills, and develop teamwork, while also strengthening the bond between teachers and students. The joyful and learning-oriented environment left lasting memories for everyone involved.
In conclusion, the Culture Day celebration organized by Max Green Skill Development Organization was highly successful, colorful, and informative. The program achieved its objectives effectively and left a positive and lasting impact on all participants. Max Green Skill Development Organization remains committed to organizing such meaningful educational and cultural activities in the future for the holistic development of students and the promotion of cultural harmony.

 Human Rights Day 2024 | Tharparkar 🌍
“Where dignity is nurtured, hope finds its voice.”
On the occasion of International Human Rights Day, Max Green Skill Development Organisation, in collaboration with the Human Rights Department, Government of Sindh, proudly hosted a meaningful and inspiring event in District Tharparkar — a land of resilience, culture, and untold stories.
💚 The event brought together government representatives, academicians, civil society leaders, and youth stakeholders to reflect on the challenges and opportunities of human rights protection in Sindh, with special focus on rural and marginalized communities.
📚 Empowering Through Education
Max Green Skill Development Organisation set up interactive stalls showcasing Non-Formal Education Programs, implemented under the supervision of the Directorate of Literacy & Non-Formal Education, Government of Sindh, aimed at out-of-school children — because education is the first step toward dignity and rights.
🎖️ We were deeply honored by the presence of our Chief Guest, Honorable Rajvir Singh Sodha, whose heartfelt address emphasized justice, equality, and compassion for every citizen.
🤝 Distinguished guests who graced the occasion included:
Mr. Khalid A.K. Chachar, Secretary Human Rights Department, Government of Sindh (Focal Person for Suicide & Coordinator to SACM Ms. Nazia Naz),
Mr. Pertab Shivani, Veerji Kolhi (SACM),
Mr. Abdul Haleem Jagirani, Deputy Commissioner Tharparkar (Mithi),
Mr. Muhammad Shoaib Memon, SSP Tharparkar.
Their insightful speeches highlighted institutional responsibility, protection of vulnerable communities, and the importance of collaborative action.
🌱 A special moment of the event was the launch of youth initiatives by the Human Rights Department, aimed at developing a new generation of human rights advocates. Inspiring addresses by the DC and SSP Mithi reinforced the role of youth as agents of change.
🗣️ Panel Discussion | Voices That Matter
The thought-provoking panel included:
Mr. Munsif Lashari (CEO, Max Green SDO),
Mr. Bharu Mal Amrani (Writer),
Ms. Kamla Bheel (Vice Chairperson, District Council Tharparkar),
Mr. Ashok Kumar Odhano (Regional Head, NCHD),
Mr. Khatau Jani (Journalist),
Mr. Abass Khoso (Focal Person for Suicide),
and was skillfully moderated by Ms. Nazia Naz.
🤲 Honorable Minister Mr. Rajvir Singh Sodha Sahib, along with Mr. Munsif Lashari (CEO), Mr. Hamza Khalid Arain (Program Manager), and team members, also visited the skill development and education stalls, appreciating efforts to transform lives through learning and skills.
🎭 The event concluded with students’ powerful speeches and a moving tableau on child rights, reminding us that every child deserves protection, education, and a future full of promise.
✨ Together, we reaffirm our commitment — to human dignity, to equality, and to ensuring that no voice in Tharparkar goes unheard.

On this International Day of Persons with Disabilities, we witnessed a moment that truly touched every heart.
At the AALTP Centre of Non-Formal Education—managed by Max Green Skill Development Organisation in collaboration with the Sindh Education Foundation, Government of Sindh—a beautiful story of hope unfolded.
Rozina, a bright and determined student from Serena Society, Karachi, has been part of our learning family as a Package-B learner. Despite the challenges she faces, her passion for education and her courage to move forward have always shined through.
Understanding her struggle for mobility, our respected CEO, Mr. Munsif Lashari, stepped forward with warmth and compassion. In a heartfelt gesture, he personally handed over a wheelchair to Rozina—opening doors to new opportunities and bringing comfort to her journey ahead.
The smile on Rozina’s face said more than words ever could. It wasn’t just a wheelchair…
It was freedom.
It was dignity.
It was hope.
Today reminded us that when we support one person, we uplift an entire community. And when we stand for inclusion, we build a brighter, kinder future for all.
Max Green Skill Development Organisation remains committed to empowering learners like Rozina—ensuring that every individual, regardless of ability, has the chance to learn, grow, and thrive.
Together, we can make every day a day of possibilities.






 *A Story of Courage — Rozina’s Journey* ✨
In the heart of Serena Society, Karachi, lives a brave young girl named Rozina—a student of the AALTP Non-Formal Education Centre, managed by Max Green Skill Development Organisation in collaboration with the Sindh Education Foundation, Government of Sindh.
Rozina belongs to Package B, but her journey to education has been anything but simple.
Because of her physical disability, no school was willing to accept her. Doors kept closing… but her hope never faded.
And then came the moment that changed everything.
*Max Green Skill Development Organisation opened their doors and their hearts.*
They welcomed Rozina when the world turned her away. Today, she proudly continues her studies with us in Package B, proving that disability is not a barrier—only society’s mindset is.
Rozina may be disabled in her legs, but her spirit walks miles ahead.
Her smile, her determination, and her courage remind us every day that every child deserves a chance, a classroom, and a future full of possibilities.
This is not just Rozina’s story.
This is a message of hope, inclusion, and humanity.
Where others saw limitations…
we saw potential.





On this International Day of Persons with Disabilities, we witnessed a moment that truly touched every heart.
At the AALTP Centre of Non-Formal Education—managed by Max Green Skill Development Organisation in collaboration with the Sindh Education Foundation, Government of Sindh—a beautiful story of hope unfolded.
Rozina, a bright and determined student from Serena Society, Karachi, has been part of our learning family as a Package-B learner. Despite the challenges she faces, her passion for education and her courage to move forward have always shined through.
Understanding her struggle for mobility, our respected CEO, Mr. Munsif Lashari, stepped forward with warmth and compassion. In a heartfelt gesture, he personally handed over a wheelchair to Rozina—opening doors to new opportunities and bringing comfort to her journey ahead.
The smile on Rozina’s face said more than words ever could. It wasn’t just a wheelchair…
It was freedom.
It was dignity.
It was hope.
Today reminded us that when we support one person, we uplift an entire community. And when we stand for inclusion, we build a brighter, kinder future for all.
Max Green Skill Development Organisation remains committed to empowering learners like Rozina—ensuring that every individual, regardless of ability, has the chance to learn, grow, and thrive.
Together, we can make every day a day of possibilities.
